---
description: Adapt a published piece to another format.
argument-hint: <published path> <target format>
---

Input: $ARGUMENTS

1. Read the published piece, `formats/<target>.md`, `voice.md`, and `CLAUDE.md`.
   Check `published/<target>/` and `drafts/<target>/` for an existing version.
2. Before writing, list:
   - **What changes**: what the new medium needs that the original does not
     have (structure, pacing, spoken vs read, depth, demo moments).
   - **What gets cut**: parts that do not work in the new medium.
   - **Material needed**: extra detail, footage, screen recordings, numbers,
     or examples Sofia must supply. Be specific.
3. If the needed material is missing, stop after the list and ask for it.
   Do not invent details to fill the gap.
4. If the target format is not active, follow what its format file says.
5. Otherwise write the adaptation as a new piece, not a reformat. Save to
   `drafts/<target>/YYYY-MM-DD-<slug>.md`, linking the source in the header.
