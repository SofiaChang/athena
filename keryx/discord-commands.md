# Keryx Discord Commands

## Daily ready post

Keryx posts after note creation:

- "Your Keryx daily briefing is ready."
- Note path: `vault/Abyss/daily/news-YYYY-MM-DD.md`
- Top 3 highlights
- Follow-up hints

## Follow-up commands

- `!keryx <topic>`
  - Deep dive on topic (example: `!keryx semiconductor tariffs`)
- `!keryx story <n>`
  - Deep dive by ranked story number from latest briefing
- `!keryx watch <topic>`
  - Add topic to watchlist for future runs

## Follow-up response contract

Every deep dive response includes:

1. What changed
2. Why it matters
3. Timeline
4. Key actors
5. Risks and opportunities
6. What to watch next
7. Sources
